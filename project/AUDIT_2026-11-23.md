## Summary

The research record is in good order: every reported number traces to a record, the pass marks were frozen before the runs, failures are reported as failures, held-out data is kept out in code (encounter 276 is never in the rhythm bank or the realism library; encounter 191 is refused as noise), and the checks, CI and lineage work.

The weak point is the **public page**. `project/RELEASES.md` says it is open to anyone with the address (`"AllowFunnel": true`), and its upload and job routes have no limits a stranger cannot get around: three findings can take the server down (BUG-2 to BUG-4), and two statements in `.github/SECURITY.md` are not true of the code (BUG-6, BUG-7). Until BUG-2 to BUG-5 are fixed, the simplest protection is `"AllowFunnel": false` in `deploy/tailscale/serve.json`, which keeps the page to the owner's own devices.

**Claims that read stronger than the evidence (WC-R7a).**

- *False messages.* Each decode can check up to 8 frame starts × 3 header readings × 16 repair combinations = 384 candidate messages against a 16-bit CRC, and soft repair leaves only 2 parity symbols as an extra check. The worst case is therefore about 384 × 2⁻¹⁶ ≈ 0.6 % per decode of noise. Campaign v1 measured 0 in 7,900 tries, which bounds the rate at 0.08 % (3,700 tries) and 0.6 % (500 empty recordings). That supports "no false message was seen"; claim C4's wording "never accepts a false message" (THREAT_MODEL section 6, TRACEABILITY) reads as a guarantee. A receiver listening around the clock would decode many windows per hour. `[DECISION]` keep the frozen format for campaign v2 and state the per-decode bound beside C4; a longer check (CRC-32, or a keyed tag) belongs in a later format.
- *Timing.* THREAT_MODEL's "What may be said today" says the timing "passes as real false killer whale whistling" without the qualifier. C6 (timing inside sea noise, AUC 0.684) failed. The README says it correctly ("on its own").
- *Fresh samples.* README: "Every data whistle is a fresh sample from its component, so no shape repeats exactly." The samples are tempered (tau = 0.5), and a whistle that fails 20 self-check redraws falls back to its type's mean shape, which does repeat.

## A lead for WC-R3 (WC-R3a)

Not a bug: an observation from the mixture records that may explain the shape AUC of 0.944. In the epoch-101 mixture, 2,796 of the 3,000 components have fewer than 65 members (the latent size is 64), 220 have a single member, and 73 % of all covariance eigenvalues sit at the regularisation floor (`gmm.json` diagnostics). The codebook takes components with at least 32 members (`[codebook] min_nk = 32`), so every symbol's covariance spans at most 31 directions plus the floor, and tau = 0.5 narrows it further. Coded whistles of one type may therefore vary much less than real whistles do, which a discriminator can learn; its top clues (curve fit error, net rise, span) fit that picture. Worth measuring first in WC-R3: the spread of real whistles assigned to a codebook component against the spread of its samples.
