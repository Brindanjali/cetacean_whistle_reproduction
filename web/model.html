Imagined by

No recording needed: the machine dreams up brand-new whistles from everything it has heard. Choose how many, and if you like, ask for a certain kind.

Whistle model after Park et al., “A Generative Model for Cetacean Whistles Using Variational Autoencoder and Mixture of Gaussians,” IEEE Access 14 (2026), pp. 27877–27894. Recordings: NOAA DCLDE 2022 (HICEAS 2017), false killer whales.

Every image is a 0.68 s, ±6 kHz picture of one whistle’s shape with its centre pitch removed; the sound is a pure tone that follows it (the paper’s Fig. 2), placed back at the whistle’s own centre pitch. Real whistles also carry harmonics, loudness changes and noise; the model does not.

Falsetto: short messages hidden in these whistles.
